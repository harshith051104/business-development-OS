# ELLY BD OS — User Guide

## What is ELLY?

ELLY is your AI-powered Business Development Operating System. It manages the full commercial lifecycle: finding prospects, researching them, reaching out, and tracking deals through to close.

## Quick Start

### Login
Go to your ELLY URL → enter email + password → Dashboard loads.

### Dashboard
The Dashboard shows your pipeline at a glance:
- **Organizations** — total prospects and their stages
- **Opportunities** — deals in progress and their values
- **Upcoming Meetings** — what's scheduled this week
- **Pending Follow-ups** — tasks that need attention

---

## Core Workflow

### 1. Find Prospects (Discovery)

**Sidebar → Discovery**

Search for real companies by industry and location:
1. Enter keyword: "SaaS companies", "fintech London", "manufacturing Munich"
2. Click **Search**
3. Review results — each shows name, website, description
4. Click **Save** on companies you want to pursue
5. Saved companies become **Organization** records

### 2. Research & Qualify

**Sidebar → Organizations → click an org**

Once an org is saved:
- View their website, industry, location
- Add **Contacts** (decision-makers you'll reach out to)
- Set **Priority** (High/Medium/Low)
- Add notes about the opportunity

Use the **Copilot** (bottom-right) for AI research:
- "Research Apex Financial Cloud"
- "What tech stack does this company use?"
- "Who is the CTO?"

### 3. Contact Decision-Makers

**Sidebar → Outreach**

Two ways to contact:

**A. Individual Email**
1. Open an Organization → Contacts
2. Click a contact → **Send Email**
3. ELLY drafts a personalized email using AI
4. Review and edit the draft
5. Click **Send** → email sent via Gmail

**B. Campaign (Bulk)**
1. Sidebar → **Campaigns** → Create Campaign
2. Add contacts from multiple organizations
3. ELLY AI personalizes each email
4. Review drafts → Approve → Send sequence

### 4. Track Replies

When a prospect replies:
- ELLY detects it automatically
- Organization status updates to **Responded**
- A **Follow-up** is created
- An **Opportunity** is auto-created

You'll see this in:
- **Dashboard** — pipeline updates
- **Daily Priorities** — "Prospect Replied" appears as top priority
- **Communications** — reply logged

### 5. Run Meetings

**Sidebar → Meetings → Schedule**

1. Create a meeting → link to Organization + Contact
2. Meeting shows in **Daily Priorities** as "Next Meeting"
3. After meeting → click **Complete**
4. Add notes, decisions, next actions
5. Auto-creates follow-up task

### 6. Demo & Proposal

After a successful meeting:
1. **Demo** → Schedule and record demo outcome
2. **Proposal** → Create proposal with value, scope, terms
3. Both advance the opportunity stage automatically

### 7. Negotiate & Close

1. **Negotiation** → Track issues, requirements, close date
2. When agreed → mark as **Agreed**
3. Opportunity moves to **Customer** stage
4. **Customer** record created with contract value and renewal date

### 8. ROI Tracking

The **Reports** section shows:
- Pipeline value by stage
- Win/loss rates
- Revenue won
- Campaign performance (emails sent → replies → meetings → deals)

## AI Copilot

The Copilot (bottom-right chat) handles natural language:

| Say this | ELLY does this |
|----------|---------------|
| "Find fintech companies in London" | Runs Discovery search |
| "Research Apex Financial" | Gathers company intel |
| "Draft email to Sarah Chen" | Creates personalized draft |
| "What are my top priorities today?" | Shows Daily Priorities |
| "Create meeting with Fortis Healthcare" | Schedules meeting |
| "Show pipeline value" | Displays opportunity stats |

---

## Daily Priorities

**Sidebar → Priorities**

ELLY ranks what to do today:
1. **Who to contact** — high-priority orgs with no outreach
2. **Overdue follow-ups** — tasks past due
3. **Next meeting** — upcoming meeting to prepare for
4. **Top opportunity** — highest-value deal to advance
5. **Stale deals** — opportunities with no activity in 14+ days

---

## Email Safety

- ELLY **drafts** emails but **never sends autonomously**
- You always review before clicking Send
- Rate limit: 20 emails/hour (prevents Gmail blocking)
- All sends are logged in Communications + Audit Log

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|

| `G` then `O` | Go to Organizations |
| `G` then `C` | Go to Contacts |

## Tips

- **Use Discovery first** — don't manually create orgs, let ELLY find them
- **Check Priorities daily** — ELLY ranks what matters most
- **Complete meetings promptly** — triggers follow-ups and stage advances
- **Keep notes updated** — helps Copilot give better suggestions
- **Use Campaigns for outreach** — AI personalization beats generic emails
